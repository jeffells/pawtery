🐾 Kathie's Website Cheat Sheet

Simple steps for managing your Pawtery website
1

How to Log In to Your Admin Panel

→ Open any web browser (Chrome, Safari, etc.)
→ Type this address in the bar at the top:
pawtery.shop/admin
→ Enter your email: feebiejean@gmail.com
→ Enter your password and click "Log in"
→ You'll see your dashboard with "Products", "Site Settings", and "Market Dates"
2

How to Add a New Product

→ Log in (see Step 1 above)
→ Click "Products" in the left menu
→ Click the "New Products" button (top right)
→ Fill in: Product Name, Price, and Short Description
→ Click the photo box → click "Choose an image" → pick a photo from your computer
→ Write the Story (this is the personal note that pops up when someone clicks the item)
→ Set the Sort Order number (1 = shows first, 2 = shows second, etc.)
→ Make sure "Visible on site" is turned ON (green)
→ Click "Save" at the top right — your new item will appear on the website in about 60 seconds!
Tip: For best photo results, take photos in natural daylight, lay the piece on a white cloth or light wood surface, and shoot straight down or at a slight angle. Square photos work best.
3

How to Edit or Update an Existing Product

→ Log in and click "Products"
→ Click the product you want to change
→ Make your changes (price, photo, description — anything)
→ Click "Save" — changes are live in about 60 seconds
To hide a product temporarily: Click the item → turn off "Visible on site" → Save. It disappears from the website but is saved for later.
4

How to Add an Upcoming Market Date

→ Log in and click "Market Dates & Events"
→ Click "New Events"
→ Fill in: Event Name, Date, Location, and any Notes
→ Click "Save"
5

How to Update Your About Story or Announcement

→ Log in and click "Site Settings"
→ Click "Contact & About"
→ Edit the text in any box — About story, announcement bar, etc.
→ Click "Save"
6

If You Forget Your Password

→ Go to pawtery.shop/admin
→ Click "Forgot password?"
→ Enter feebiejean@gmail.com
→ Check your email and follow the reset link
Need more help? Your website was built and set up for you. For any questions or changes beyond this sheet, reach out to whoever set this up for you, or email Netlify support at netlify.com/support (they're free and very helpful).
